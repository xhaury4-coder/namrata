Bas Ek Hi

Ek simple message se shuru hui kahani.
Jo attachment, control, aur khud ko kho dene tak le gayi.

01

Shaam ka Waqt

Shaam ka waqt tha.

IIT campus ki sadkein usual se thodi zyada shaant thi. Pedon ke patton se halki si awaaz aa rahi thi, aur hawa me ek ajeeb sa sukoon ghula hua tha. Suraj dheere dheere dhal raha tha, aur uski halki orange roshni ground par padh rahi thi.

Main aur mera dost Abhay bina kisi khaas baat ke walk kar rahe the.

Sach kahu to us waqt meri zindagi bilkul normal thi. Na koi bada goal, na koi bada stress. Haan, thodi padhai thi, thoda coding ka interest tha, aur thodi si apni duniya.

Main un logon me se tha jo emotions ko itna seriously nahi lete. Pyaar-vyaar mere liye bas ek concept tha — movies aur reels tak limited.

Mujhe lagta tha real life me aisa kuch hota hi nahi.

Par shayad zindagi ko irony pasand hoti hai.

Tabhi Abhay ne ekdum se bola, “Bhai tujhe pata hai Utkarsh ki crush yahi IIT me hai?”

Maine bina zyada interest ke pucha, “Accha? Kaun hai?”

Usne apna phone nikala, screen unlock ki, aur meri taraf badhaya.

“Ye dekh.”

Maine casually screen ki taraf dekha…

Aur us ek second me sab kuch change ho gaya.

Screen par ek ladki thi. Simple. Bohot simple. Na koi heavy makeup, na koi show-off wali pose. Bas ek normal si photo. Par us normal me hi kuch alag tha.

Uski aankhon me ek ajeeb si shanti thi. Jaise wo kisi aur hi world me rehti ho. Jaise usse duniya ke chaos se koi matlab hi nahi.

Main bas usse dekhte reh gaya. Time literally slow ho gaya tha.

“Bhai?” Abhay ne hila kar bola, “Kya hua?”

Main ekdum se reality me aaya aur turant nazar hata li. “Kuch nahi,” maine casual banne ki koshish ki.

Par andar kuch ho chuka tha. Dil me ek ajeeb si feeling aayi — jo maine pehle kabhi feel nahi ki thi.

Par maine turant apne aap ko control kiya. “Dost ki crush hai,” maine khud se kaha. “Door reh.”

Ye line maine sirf bola nahi… main genuinely maanna chahta tha.

Hum aage chalne lage, par main physically waha tha… mentally nahi. Mera dimaag baar baar usi face par ja raha tha.

Wo simplicity… wo innocence… wo calmness… Aisa lag raha tha jaise maine kuch aisa dekh liya ho jo mujhe nahi dekhna chahiye tha.

Walk khatam hui. Hum ghar ki taraf laut rahe the.

Tabhi Abhay ne ek aur bomb gira diya. “Waise,” usne casually bola, “wo Utkarsh ki bandi nahi hai.”

Maine turant uski taraf dekha, “Matlab?”

“Bandi nahi hai… bas crush hai.”

Us ek line ne sab kuch change kar diya.

Jo cheez main khud se rok raha tha… usko ekdum se green signal mil gaya.

Maine kuch nahi bola. Par andar ek awaaz aayi — “To fir problem kya hai?”

Ghar pahunchne tak main sirf uske baare me soch raha tha. Uska naam kya hoga? Kaise baat karti hogi? Real life me bhi utni hi simple hogi?

Main apne room me aaya. Phone uthaya. Instagram open kiya. Search bar me uska username type kiya. Profile khuli.

Dil ki dhadkan thodi fast ho gayi. Main uski photos scroll karne laga. Har photo me wo same thi — simple, calm, innocent. Koi fake energy nahi.

Aur shayad wahi cheez mujhe sabse zyada attract kar rahi thi.

Main uski profile pe lagbhag 20–25 minute tak bas scroll karta raha. Har baar sochta — “Bas ye last photo.” Par phir next open kar leta.

Us waqt mujhe realise nahi hua… Par main already usme invest ho raha tha. Emotionally.

Main phone side me rakhne hi wala tha ki meri nazar ek cheez par ruk gayi.

Message

Bas ek button. Itna simple. Par us button ke peeche kitni possibilities thi… Ya to wo ignore karegi… Ya reply karegi… Ya block bhi kar sakti hai.

Maine phone haath me pakda aur khud se baat karne laga. “Message karu?” “Pagal hai kya? Kya bolega?” “Hi bol de…” “Bhai awkward ho jayega…”

Main literally 10–15 minute tak sirf isi internal debate me laga raha. Aakhir me maine ek deep breath li. Aur decide kiya — “Jo hoga dekha jayega.”

Maine chat open ki. Keyboard khula. Fingers thodi shaky thi. Maine type kiya —

Hi

Bas ek word. Na koi introduction. Na koi excuse. Bas ek simple “Hi”.

Maine kuch seconds tak us message ko dekha. Socha — “Send karu ya nahi?” Dil zor zor se dhadak raha tha.

Phir maine aankhen band ki… aur send kar diya. Message chala gaya.

Aur us moment me… meri kahani officially shuru ho gayi.

Mujhe nahi pata tha ki ye ek simple “Hi” mujhe kaha le jayega… Par itna zaroor tha — Ab main wapas nahi ja sakta tha.

02

Seen Nahi Aaya

Message bhejne ke baad sab kuch ajeeb sa ho gaya tha. Room wahi tha. Light wahi thi. Phone bhi wahi tha. Par main… same nahi tha.

Screen par wo chhota sa “Hi” likha hua tha… aur uske neeche “Seen” bhi nahi aaya tha.

Main bas us chat ko dekh raha tha. Jaise agar main usse dekhte rahunga to reply jaldi aa jayega.

5 minute beet gaye. Koi reply nahi. Maine phone lock kiya… bed par phek diya… aur khud ko distract karne ki koshish ki. Par distraction naam ki cheez exist hi nahi kar rahi thi.

Har 20–30 second me main phone uthata… unlock karta… chat open karta… aur fir wahi blank screen dekh kar wapas rakh deta. Ye cycle repeat hoti rahi.

“Bhai tu pagal ho gaya hai,” maine khud se kaha. Par dil maan nahi raha tha.

10 minute beet gaye. Ab thoda regret feel hone laga. “Message nahi karna chahiye tha… kya zarurat thi?” dimaag bol raha tha. “Ek hi to message hai… kya bigad gaya?” dil defend kar raha tha.

Main bed par seedha let gaya… ceiling ko dekhne laga. Aur uske baare me sochne laga. Kya wo abhi online hogi? Kya usne message dekha hoga? Kya wo ignore kar rahi hai? Ya fir bas busy hai?

Har possibility mere dimaag me chal rahi thi.

Tabhi phone vibrate hua. Ek second ke liye mera dil literally skip kar gaya. Main turant uth kar phone pakda. Notification bar me uska naam tha.

Maine bina delay kiye chat open ki.

Hello :)

Bas itna sa reply. Par us ek smiley ne jo feel diya… wo main explain nahi kar sakta. Jaise kisi ne andar ek switch on kar diya ho.

Maine turant type kiya — “Kaise ho?” Phir backspace kiya. “Hi” likha. Fir delete kiya. Main overthink kar raha tha. “Normal reh,” maine khud ko bola.

Finally maine likha — “Hey”. Send. Ab ball uske court me thi.

Is baar reply thoda jaldi aaya. “I’m good, tum?” Conversation start ho chuki thi. Aur dheere dheere wo awkwardness khatam hone lagi.

Humne basic baatein ki — school, class, kaha rehte ho, kya karte ho… Par ek cheez mujhe turant feel hui… Wo baat karne me alag thi. Uska tone soft tha. Wo jaldi irritate nahi hoti thi. Aur sabse important — wo genuinely reply karti thi. Sirf formal nahi.

Main har message ko dhyan se padh raha tha… jaise uske words ke peeche kuch aur meaning dhund raha hoon. Aur shayad wo bhi effort daal rahi thi.

Conversation dheere dheere flow me aane lagi. Time ka pata hi nahi chala. 30 minutes… 1 hour… 2 hours… Hum lagatar baat kar rahe the.

Beech me ek baar usne likha, “Tum interesting ho.” Main screen ko dekh kar muskura diya. Kisi ne pehli baar aisa bola tha mujhe. Us ek line ne mujhe aur invest kar diya.

Raat ho gayi thi. Ghar me sab so chuke the. Light off thi… bas phone ki screen hi room ko light de rahi thi. Aur main uske saath baat kar raha tha. Wo moment simple tha… par special tha.

Aakhir me usne likha, “Mujhe ab sona hai”. Maine reply kiya, “Haan same”. Par sach ye tha — mujhe bilkul neend nahi aa rahi thi.

Usne last message bheja — “Good night :)”. Maine bhi likha — “Good night”. Par send karne ke baad bhi main chat band nahi kar pa raha tha. Main us chat ko baar baar open karta… uske messages padhta… uska “:)” dekhta… Aur khud se ek hi baat bolta — “Yaar ye alag hai.”

Us raat main late soya. Aur sone se pehle last thought kya tha? “Kal fir baat hogi.”

Agli subah main usual se jaldi uth gaya. Sabse pehle kya kiya? Phone check. Uska message nahi tha. Thoda ajeeb laga. Par fir maine khud message kiya — “Good morning”. 5 minute me reply aa gaya — “Good morning :)”

Aur us moment me mujhe realise hua… Ye sirf ek conversation nahi rahi. Ye ek routine ban rahi hai.

Din bhar hum thodi thodi der me baat karte rahe. Koi special topic nahi tha… Par fir bhi baat interesting lag rahi thi. Aur dheere dheere… wo mere din ka important part banne lagi.

Agar 1–2 ghante reply nahi aata tha… To thoda uneasy feel hota tha. Main check karta — “Online thi kya?” “Story dali kya?” Main khud ko justify karta — “Bas aise hi check kar raha hoon”. Par sach ye tha… Main uski aadat dal raha tha.

Aur aadatein dangerous hoti hain. Shuru me wo comfort deti hain… Aur jab chhoot jati hain… To tod deti hain.

Par us waqt… Main bas enjoy kar raha tha. Mujhe nahi pata tha ki main dheere dheere ek aise loop me ja raha hoon… Jahan se nikalna itna aasaan nahi hoga.

03

Habit se Need

Shuru me sab normal tha. Ek “Hi”, ek “Hello”, thodi si baat… aur bas. Par dheere dheere… wo “thodi si baat” badh kar “poora din” ban gayi.

Ab situation ye thi ki din ki shuruat uske message se hoti thi… aur end bhi usi par. Subah alarm se pehle aankh khulti thi… par uthne ka reason alarm nahi, uska “Good morning” check karna hota tha. Aur agar kabhi uska message nahi hota tha… To main khud kar deta tha.

Good morning :)

Shuru me ye sirf ek routine lag raha tha. Par dheere dheere… ye routine ek habit ban gaya. Aur fir… Habit ek need.

Main school me hota tha… par dhyaan phone par hota tha. Teacher kuch padhate rehte… aur main bench ke niche phone chhupa kar uske messages read karta. Kabhi kabhi to bina wajah hi message kar deta — “Kya kar rahi ho?” Aur jab wo reply karti — “Kuch nahi” To bhi mujhe accha lagta tha. Kyuki baat uske reply ki nahi thi… Baat uski presence ki thi. Uska online hona hi enough lagta tha.

Ek din usne casual sa message kiya, “Tum itna message kyu karte ho?” Main thoda awkward ho gaya. Maine likha, “Bas aise hi…” Par sach ye tha — mujhe khud nahi pata tha kyu. Ya shayad pata tha… par main accept nahi kar raha tha.

Mujhe uski aadat pad chuki thi. Aur aadat aur pyaar ke beech ek thin line hoti hai… Jo main dheere dheere cross kar raha tha.

Dheere dheere humari baatein aur personal hone lagi. Ab sirf “kya kar rahe ho” tak limited nahi thi. Hum apni daily life share karne lage. Usne mujhe apne ghar ke baare me bataya… apni family ke baare me… apni chhoti chhoti problems ke baare me. Aur main? Main bhi usse sab batane laga. Wo cheezein jo main normally kisi se share nahi karta tha.

Ek comfort zone ban gaya tha. Aisa lagta tha ki wo mujhe judge nahi karegi. Aur shayad wahi cheez mujhe sabse zyada connect kar rahi thi.

Ek raat hum late night baat kar rahe the. Random topics chal rahe the. Tabhi usne ek question pucha, “Tum itna care kyu karte ho?”

Maine turant likha, “Kyuki tum important ho.” Message send karne ke baad main kuch seconds tak screen ko dekhte raha. Ye pehli baar tha jab maine indirectly accept kiya tha… Ki wo mere liye special hai.

Usne reply me sirf ek emoji bheja — “🙂” Par us emoji me bhi ek softness thi. Aur us moment me mujhe laga… Shayad wo bhi same feel kar rahi hai.

Ab main uski har chhoti cheez notice karne laga tha. Wo kab online aati hai. Kab offline hoti hai. Kis time reply karti hai. Kis tone me baat karti hai. Agar uska reply thoda late aata… To main turant sochne lagta — “Kisi aur se baat kar rahi hogi kya?”

Ye thoughts chhote the… Par dangerous the. Kyuki yahi se insecurity start hoti hai. Par us waqt mujhe ye sab normal lag raha tha. Mujhe lagta tha — “Main bas care karta hoon”.

Ek din wo thodi upset lag rahi thi. Replies short the. Tone thoda cold tha. Maine pucha, “Sab thik hai?” Usne bola, “Haan”. Par mujhe pata tha — kuch to galat hai. Main baar baar puchta raha. Finally usne bola, “Bas mood off hai”.

Us moment me mujhe realise hua… Uska mood directly mera mood affect karta hai. Agar wo khush hoti… main khush hota. Agar wo sad hoti… main restless ho jata. Aur yahi wo point tha jahan maine apni emotions ka control uske haath me de diya. Bina realise kiye.

Ab main uski life me zyada involved hone laga tha. Usne kya khaya, kaha gayi, kis se baat ki… Mujhe sab jaana hota tha. Aur agar wo kuch nahi batati… To mujhe ajeeb sa feel hota. Jaise kuch missing ho. Jaise main uski life ka part nahi hoon. Aur dheere dheere… main usse expect karne laga ki wo har cheez share kare. Par problem ye thi… Maine ye kabhi clearly bola nahi. Bas andar hi andar expect karta raha. Aur jab expectations meet nahi hoti… To frustration build hota hai.

Ek aur change aaya tha. Main apni life ignore karne laga tha. Coding jo mujhe itni pasand thi… Wo side me chali gayi. Padhai me bhi mann nahi lag raha tha. Friends ke saath time spend karna kam ho gaya. Main har free moment me uske saath chat karna chahta tha. Aur mujhe lagta tha — “Ye normal hai”. Par aaj sochta hoon… Wo normal nahi tha. Wo attachment tha. Strong attachment. Jo dheere dheere obsession me convert ho raha tha.

Ek din usne casually bola, “Tum na thode alag ho”. Maine pucha, “Kaise?” Wo boli, “Pata nahi… bas ho”. Main muskura diya. Par andar se main aur deep chala gaya. Maine us line ko apne hisaab se interpret kiya — “Wo mujhe pasand karti hai”. Aur shayad wahi meri sabse badi galti thi. Maine assume karna start kar diya. Aur pyaar me assumptions… Sabse dangerous cheez hoti hain.

Ab main uske bina apna din imagine nahi kar pata tha. Agar wo 2–3 ghante reply na kare… To main uneasy ho jata. Phone baar baar check karta. Last seen dekhta. Story check karta. Aur khud ko bolta — “Bas aise hi dekh raha hoon”. Par sach ye tha… Main dependent ho chuka tha. Emotionally. Aur dependency kabhi healthy nahi hoti.

Par us waqt… Mujhe sirf ek cheez feel ho rahi thi — “Main usse pyaar karta hoon”. Aur shayad… Ye sach bhi tha. Par ye pyaar… Pure nahi tha. Isme fear tha. Insecurity thi. Aur sabse dangerous — Control ki shuruat thi.

04

Utkarsh

Sab kuch dheere dheere settle ho raha tha. Main uski aadat ban chuka tha… aur wo meri. Din normal lagte the, par andar se har cheez uske around revolve karti thi. Uska ek message poora mood change kar deta tha. Uski ek smiley poora din bana deti thi. Aur uska thoda sa late reply… anxiety de deta tha. Par mujhe lag raha tha — “Ye hi pyaar hai”. Main khush tha. Ya shayad… main khud ko convince kar raha tha ki main khush hoon.

Phir ek din… Sab kuch change ho gaya. Usne message kiya, “Ek baat bolu?” Maine turant reply kiya, “Haan bolo”. Usne thoda time liya. Phir likha, “Tum Utkarsh ko jaante ho na?”

Mera dil ek second ke liye ruk gaya. Naam familiar tha. Abhay ki wajah se. Maine reply kiya, “Haan thoda bahut… kyu?” Typing… Stop… Typing… Stop… Phir uska message aaya, “Wo mujhe pasand karta hai”.

Maine screen ko kuch seconds tak dekha. Ye baat mujhe already pata thi… par uske muh se sunna alag feel tha. Maine normal banne ki koshish ki, “To?” Usne likha, “Usse pata chal gaya hai ki hum baat karte hain”.

Ab mere andar ek ajeeb sa pressure build hone laga. Maine pucha, “To problem kya hai?” Usne reply diya, “Wo thoda aggressive hai… mujhe darr lag raha hai”.

Us message ke baad kuch seconds ke liye main blank ho gaya. Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha tha kya feel karu. Gussa? Dar? Jealousy? Sab mix ho gaya tha.

Us raat main pehli baar seriously sochne laga… “Kya main galat kar raha hoon?” Ek taraf wo thi… Jiski aadat mujhe pad chuki thi. Dusri taraf ye situation… Jo clearly simple nahi thi. Main easily peeche hat sakta tha. Bol sakta tha — “Tu safe reh, main door ho jata hoon”. Par problem ye thi… Main ab door nahi ho sakta tha. Main already involved ho chuka tha. Emotionally. Deep.

Agle din Abhay mila. Usne casually bola, “Bhai Utkarsh ko pata chal gaya hai tum dono ke baare me”. Maine normal banne ki koshish ki, “To?” Wo bola, “Wo thoda gunda type hai… sambhal ke reh”. Ye sunte hi mere andar halka sa darr aaya. Par us darr ke saath ek aur cheez bhi aayi… Ego. “Mujhe kya karega?” maine socha. Par sach ye tha… Mujhe pata nahi tha wo kya kar sakta hai.

Us din ke baad situation change hone lagi. Uske messages me tension aane lagi. Wo pehle jaisi free nahi thi. Kabhi kabhi wo bolti, “Aaj baat nahi kar paungi”. Kabhi bolti, “Thoda careful rehna”. Aur ye sab sun kar mera mind aur restless hone laga. Mujhe lagne laga… “Koi beech me aa raha hai”. Aur mujhe ye bilkul pasand nahi tha.

Ek shaam main ghar ke bahar tha. Phone pocket me tha. Tabhi ek unknown number se call aayi. Maine receive ki. “Tu hi hai na?” ek heavy si awaaz aayi. Maine pucha, “Kaun?” Wo bola, “Utkarsh bol raha hoon”.

Us ek line ne meri body me ek chill si daal di.

Main kuch seconds ke liye chup ho gaya. Phir maine normal banne ki koshish ki, “Haan bol”. Usne seedha bola, “Usse door reh”. Simple. Straight. Without hesitation. Maine kuch seconds socha… Phir bola, “Kyu?” Wo hasa. Aur bola, “Samajh ja… warna samjhana padega”. Call cut.

Main wahi khada reh gaya. Phone haath me tha… par dimaag kahi aur chala gaya tha.

Us raat main so nahi paya. Main baar baar same cheez soch raha tha… “Kya karu?” Option simple tha — Ya to peeche hat jao… Ya saamne khade ho jao. Par problem ye thi… Peeche hatne ka matlab tha usse khona. Aur saamne khade hone ka matlab… Unknown risk.

Maine usse message kiya, “Utkarsh ne call kiya tha”. Uska reply turant aaya, “Kya bola?” Maine sab bataya. Thodi der ke liye wo offline ho gayi. Phir uska message aaya, “Please koi panga mat lena”. Us line me darr clearly feel ho raha tha. Aur us moment me mujhe realise hua… Ye sirf meri story nahi thi. Wo bhi iss situation me fasi hui thi.

Us moment me maine ek decision liya. Main darr ke peeche nahi hatunga. Par main stupid bhi nahi banunga. Main situation ko samajh kar handle karunga. Par andar se ek aur sach tha… Main ye sab uske liye kar raha tha. Aur shayad… Khud ke liye bhi. Kyuki ab ye sirf baat nahi rahi thi. Ye ego, attachment aur pyaar ka mix ban chuka tha.

Kuch din tak sab thoda normal raha. Par tension hawa me thi. Har message ke peeche ek fear tha. Har call ke peeche ek doubt. Aur har din ek question ke saath start hota tha — “Aaj kya hone wala hai?”

Phir ek din… Wo sach me mere ghar ke bahar aa gaya. Main andar tha. Gate ke bahar awaaz aayi, “Bahar aa”. Mera heart fast beat karne laga. Main bahar gaya. Wo waha khada tha… apne ek dost ke saath. Uski aankhon me ek alag hi attitude tha. Wo seedha mere paas aaya. Aur bola, “Sach bata… wo teri girlfriend hai?”

Us moment me time slow ho gaya. Mere paas do option the — Jhoot bol du… aur sab khatam kar du. Ya sach bolu… aur jo hoga face karu. Maine uski aankhon me dekha… Aur bola, “Haan”.

Usne halka sa smirk kiya. Apne dost ki taraf dekha. Phir bola, “Record ho gaya”. Mera dimaag ek second ke liye blank ho gaya. Usne aage bola, “Ab seedha rehna… warna ghar tak baat pahunch jayegi”. Aur wo waha se chala gaya.

Main wahi khada reh gaya. Shock me. Fear me. Aur confusion me. Par ek cheez clear thi… Game ab serious ho chuka tha. Aur ab… Wapas jaane ka option almost khatam ho chuka tha.

05

Pehli Mulaqat

Us din ke baad sab kuch thoda heavy ho gaya tha. Utkarsh ka aana, uska threaten karna, recording wali baat… Ye sab mere dimaag me baar baar repeat ho raha tha. Par life rukti nahi hai. Aur pyaar bhi nahi.

Main usse baat karta raha. Aur wo bhi karti rahi. Shuru ke kuch din hum dono thode cautious the. Har message me ek hesitation hoti thi. Jaise dono ko pata ho ki situation simple nahi hai… Par fir bhi dono us situation ko ignore karne ki koshish kar rahe the.

Ek raat maine usse pucha, “Darr lag raha hai?” Usne thodi der baad reply kiya, “Haan… par tum ho na”. Us ek line ne mere andar kuch change kar diya. Pehli baar mujhe feel hua… Ki main sirf usse pasand nahi karta. Main uske liye responsible feel karne laga hoon. Jaise uski safety, uski khushi… sab kuch mere upar hai. Aur wahi se ek aur dangerous cheez start hui… Over-responsibility.

Maine usse promise kiya, “Main hoon, kuch nahi hoga”. Sach ye tha… Mujhe khud nahi pata tha kya hoga. Par us moment me main usse bas safe feel karwana chahta tha. Aur shayad… Khud ko bhi.

Dheere dheere tension thodi kam hone lagi. Utkarsh side me ho gaya… ya shayad temporarily chup ho gaya. Aur hum… Phir se normal hone lage. Par is baar kuch alag tha. Connection aur deep ho gaya tha. Jaise hum dono ne ek problem face ki ho… aur usse pass ho gaye ho. Aur aise moments relationship ko strong bana dete hain.

Ab hum sirf chat tak limited nahi rahe. Humne decide kiya — milte hain. Pehli baar. Real life me.

Us din main unusual nervous tha. Kapde 3 baar change kiye. Mirror me khud ko check karta raha. “Normal lag raha hoon na?” main khud se puch raha tha. Par andar se heart fast beat kar raha tha.

Main location par thoda jaldi pahunch gaya. Wo abhi tak nahi aayi thi. Main idhar udhar dekh raha tha. Har ladki ko dekh kar lag raha tha — “Kya ye wo hai?” Phir achanak… Wo saamne se aati hui dikhi. Same simplicity. Same innocence. Bas is baar screen ke peeche nahi… real me. Aur sach bolu… Wo photos se bhi zyada acchi thi. Mera dimaag literally blank ho gaya. Main kya bolu… kaise react karu… kuch samajh nahi aa raha tha.

Wo mere paas aayi. Thoda sa smile ki. Aur boli, “Hi”. Maine bhi awkward sa “Hi” bola. Kuch seconds tak dono chup rahe. Fir hum slowly walk karne lage. Shuru me thodi awkwardness thi… Par dheere dheere wo khatam ho gayi. Aur fir… Wahi vibe jo chat me thi… Real life me bhi aa gayi.

Humne random baatein ki. School, teachers, daily life… Par beech beech me chhoti chhoti cheezein thi jo special thi. Jaise uska bina wajah muskura dena… Jaise mera usse dekhte rehna… Jaise beech me silence bhi comfortable lagna… Ye sab normal nahi hota. Ye connection hota hai.

Us din ke baad milna routine ban gaya. Coaching ke baad hum saath me ghar jaate. Main apni coaching khatam karke uska wait karta. Kabhi 10 minute… Kabhi 1 ghanta… Kabhi usse bhi zyada. Par mujhe kabhi bura nahi lagta tha. Kyuki wait karne ka reason special tha.

Ek baar ka scene mujhe aaj bhi yaad hai. Mere pet me bohot dard ho raha tha. Itna ki khada rehna mushkil tha. Par fir bhi main uski coaching ke bahar wait kar raha tha. Time pass ho raha tha. 1 ghanta… 2 ghante… Wo nahi aayi. Phir pata chala — wo us din coaching hi nahi aayi thi. Main waha se dheere dheere ghar gaya. Dard se bhi… aur thoda dil se bhi. Par maine usse kuch nahi bola. Kyuki main usse blame nahi karna chahta tha. Main bas usse khush dekhna chahta tha. Chahe usme meri inconvenience hi kyu na ho.

Ab hum aur close ho gaye the. Main usse “Betu” bulane laga. Wo pehle thoda hasi… phir usse bhi wo naam pasand aa gaya. “Pagal ho tum,” wo bolti thi. Aur main hasta tha.

Par is perfect phase ke beech me… Ek subtle change bhi ho raha tha. Jo us waqt mujhe feel nahi hua. Par aaj clearly dikhta hai. Main usse zyada control karne laga tha. “Yaha mat jao” “Usse baat mat karo” “Late mat raho”. Mujhe lagta tha — main care kar raha hoon. Par reality me… Main uski freedom limit kar raha tha.

Ek baar maine usse bola, “Shaam ke baad mat nahana, bimar ho jaogi”. Wo hasi. Par us hasi ke peeche ek slight irritation bhi tha. Jo maine ignore kar diya.

Main uski life ka har decision influence karna chahta tha. Aur mujhe lagta tha — “Ye normal hai”. Par sach me… Ye normal nahi tha. Ye attachment ka next stage tha. Jahan insaan pyaar ke naam par control justify karta hai.

Us waqt sab perfect lag raha tha. Daily milna… Daily baat karna… Daily care… Daily “Betu”. Par shayad… Perfect cheezein zyada der perfect nahi rehti. Aur jo cheez jitni fast banti hai… Wo utni hi fast toot bhi sakti hai.

Mujhe nahi pata tha… Ki jo phase main enjoy kar raha hoon… Wo actually storm se pehle ki shanti hai. Aur jo “Betu” main itne pyaar se bol raha hoon… Ek din wahi naam mujhe tod dega.

06

Distance

Har kahani me ek phase aata hai… Jab sab kuch theek hote hue bhi theek nahi hota. Humare saath bhi wahi ho raha tha. Upar se sab normal lag raha tha. Hum mil rahe the. Baat kar rahe the. “Hasi mazak” bhi chal raha tha. Par andar hi andar… Kuch change ho raha tha. Aur shayad sabse pehle wo change usme aaya.

Maine pehli baar notice kiya… Uske replies thode late aane lage. Pehle wo 2 minute me reply karti thi… Ab 10–15 minute lag jate the. Kabhi kabhi to 1 ghanta. Shuru me maine ignore kiya. “Might be busy,” maine khud ko samjhaya. Par jab ye cheez daily hone lagi… To dikkat feel hone lagi.

Ek din maine casually pucha, “Busy rehti ho aaj kal?” Usne simple reply diya, “Haan thoda”. Bas. Itna hi. Na koi explanation, na koi detail. Aur ye wahi ladki thi… Jo pehle apni chhoti se chhoti baat share karti thi.

Maine us baat ko waha khatam kar diya. Par dimaag me wo atak gayi. “Kya change ho gaya?” Ye question repeat hone laga.

Dheere dheere ek aur change aaya. Uska tone. Pehle wo soft hoti thi. Ab thodi straight ho gayi thi. Kabhi kabhi to thodi rude bhi lagti thi. Main message karta, “Kya kar rahi ho?” Wo reply karti, “Kuch nahi”. Pehle is “kuch nahi” me warmth hoti thi. Ab bas words reh gaye the.

Main confuse ho raha tha. “Maine kya galat kiya?” “Mujhse koi mistake hui hai?” Main apne aap ko analyze karne laga. Har chat read karta… Har line ko decode karne ki koshish karta. Par answer nahi mil raha tha.

Phir ek din… Usne casually mention kiya, “Meri coaching me ek new friend bani hai”. Maine normal tone me pucha, “Accha? Kaun?” Usne naam bataya. Aur bola, “Uske saath time pass accha hota hai”. Us ek line ne mere andar ek ajeeb si feeling daal di. Jealousy. Par maine usse show nahi kiya. Maine bas reply kiya, “Nice”.

Us din ke baad maine notice kiya… Wo us friend ke saath zyada time spend karne lagi. Aur mere saath… Kam. Pehle wo free hote hi mujhe message karti thi. Ab wo free hote hi uske saath hoti thi. Aur mujhe tab message karti… Jab usse time milta.

Yaha se main change hone laga. Main aur zyada clingy ho gaya. Main baar baar message karta. “Online ho, reply kyu nahi kar rahi?” “Kisse baat kar rahi ho?” “Kaha ho?” Mujhe lagta tha — main bas care kar raha hoon. Par reality me… Main pressure create kar raha tha.

Ek din usne irritate ho kar bola, “Tum itna question kyu karte ho?” Main thoda hurt ho gaya. Maine bola, “Concern hota hai”. Wo boli, “Har cheez me itna involve mat hua karo”. Us line ne mujhe thoda hit kiya. Par maine fir bhi apne aap ko justify kiya. “Mujhe farak padta hai, isliye puchta hoon”.

Par sach kya tha? Mujhe control chahiye tha. Aur ye baat main accept nahi kar raha tha.

Dheere dheere fights start hone lagi. Chhoti chhoti baaton par. Kabhi reply late hone par… Kabhi uske kisi aur se baat karne par… Kabhi mere tone par… Kabhi uske tone par… Har baat issue banne lagi.

Ek din hum mil rahe the. Main thoda serious tha. Usne pucha, “Kya hua?” Maine bola, “Tum change ho gayi ho”. Wo thodi irritate ho gayi. “Main change nahi hui, tum overthink karte ho”. Main bola, “Pehle aisa nahi tha”. Wo boli, “Pehle tum aise nahi the”. Us line ne mujhe silent kar diya.

Main ghar aaya… Aur pehli baar maine seriously socha… “Sach me… main change ho gaya hoon?” Answer andar se aa raha tha — “Haan”. Par ego accept nahi kar raha tha.

Main usse lose nahi karna chahta tha. Isliye maine aur zyada effort dalna start kiya. Aur zyada messages. Aur zyada calls. Aur zyada presence. Par problem ye thi… Jitna main push kar raha tha… Wo utni door ja rahi thi.

Ek din wo poora din almost offline thi. Maine 7–8 messages kiye. Koi reply nahi. Raat me uska message aaya, “Busy thi”. Bas. Main explode ho gaya. “Mere liye 1 minute nahi tha?” “Poora din kya kar rahi thi?” “Mujhe ignore kyu kar rahi ho?” Messages pe messages.

Usne kuch der baad reply kiya, “Tum na bahut zyada ho gaye ho”. Ye line simple thi… Par impact heavy.

Us raat humari bohot badi fight hui. Words exchange hue. Tone harsh ho gayi. Aur end me… Silence.

Agle din koi message nahi. Na uska. Na mera. Par difference ye tha… Main wait kar raha tha. Aur shayad wo… Nahi.

Ye silence 1 din ka nahi tha. 2 din… 3 din… 4 din… Main restless ho gaya. Phone baar baar check karta. Notifications ka wait karta. Par kuch nahi.

Us moment me mujhe realise hua… Main kitna dependent ho chuka hoon. Aur wo… Kitni independent.

Finally maine decide kiya… Main usse milunga. Face to face. Clear karunga sab. Main uski coaching ke bahar gaya. Wait kiya. Wo aayi. Usne mujhe dekha. Par uske face par wo wali smile nahi thi. Wo ruk gayi. Main uske paas gaya. “Kya ho raha hai?” maine pucha.

Usne seedha bola, “Bhaiya ne chats dekh li hai”. Mera dimaag ek second ke liye blank ho gaya. “Ab?” maine dheere se pucha. Wo boli, “Ab baat mat karo”. Bas. Itna hi.

Us moment me sab kuch ruk gaya. Jo connection itna strong lag raha tha… Wo ek line me weak pad gaya. Main usse dekh raha tha. Par uski aankhon me wo feelings nahi thi. Ya shayad… Wo hide kar rahi thi. Mujhe samajh nahi aaya.

Wo waha se chali gayi. Aur main wahi khada reh gaya. Same jagah. Same insaan. Par andar se… Bilkul alag.

Us din mujhe pehli baar feel hua… Ki shayad… Main usse lose kar raha hoon. Aur sabse badi baat… Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha tha kaise rokhu.

07

It’s Over

Us din ke baad sab kuch badal gaya. Pehle humari baat har roz hoti thi… Ab silence har roz hone laga. Aur silence bhi wo wala nahi jo comfortable hota hai… Ye wala silence heavy tha. Aisa silence jisme har second ek sawal hota hai — “Kyu?”

Pehla din… Maine socha — “Shayad busy hogi”. Dusra din… “Mood off hoga”. Teesra din… “Mujhse naraz hai”. Chautha din… “Mujhe ignore kar rahi hai”. Aur chauthay din ke baad… Maine accept kar liya — “Wo baat nahi karna chahti”.

Par accept karna aur maan lena… Do alag cheezein hoti hain. Main accept kar raha tha… Par maan nahi pa raha tha.

Main har din uski coaching ke bahar jaane laga. Har din. Chahe mera koi important kaam ho ya na ho. Chahe ghar me koi dikkat ho ya na ho. Mere liye bas ek cheez important thi — “Usse ek baar dekhna”.

Main gate ke bahar khada rehta. Log aate jaate rehte. Time pass hota rehta. Par mera focus bas ek cheez par hota — “Wo kab aayegi”. Kabhi wo aati… Kabhi nahi. Aur jab aati… To mujhe avoid karti. Jaise main waha hoon hi nahi.

Ek din finally maine usse roka. “Please baat kar lo,” maine dheere se bola. Wo ruk gayi. Usne mujhe dekha. Par us nazar me wo warmth nahi thi. Jo kabhi hua karti thi.

Maine bola, “Kya ho gaya hai?” Wo chup rahi. Maine fir bola, “Mujhse koi galti hui hai?” Usne thoda irritate ho kar bola, “Ab kya baat karni hai?” Us tone ne mujhe andar tak hit kiya. Par main rukha nahi. “Mujhe bas samajhna hai…”

Kuch seconds tak silence raha. Phir usne seedha bola, “Mujhe relationship me nahi rehna”. Maine turant pucha, “Kyu?” Usne bina hesitation ke bola —

“Tum toxic ho”

Wo 3 words… Simple the. Par unhone mujhe tod diya. Main kuch seconds tak usse dekhta raha. Jaise mujhe samajh hi nahi aaya ho ki usne kya bola.

“Maine kya kiya?” maine dheere se pucha. Wo boli, “Tum har cheez control karte ho”. “Har baat me question karte ho”. “Mujhe space nahi dete”. Har line ke saath mujhe realise ho raha tha… Wo galat nahi bol rahi. Par us waqt main ye accept nahi kar pa raha tha.

“Maine sab tumhare liye kiya,” maine almost rohte hue bola. Wo boli, “Mujhe nahi chahiye tha itna”.

Us line ne sab kuch clear kar diya. Jo cheez main pyaar samajh raha tha… Wo uske liye burden ban chuki thi.

Maine uske saamne haath jod diye. Literally. “Please ek chance de do,” maine bola. “Main change ho jaunga”. “Main kuch nahi bolunga”. “Bas mat jao”.

Log dekh rahe the. Road par log ruk kar dekh rahe the. Par us waqt mujhe koi farak nahi pad raha tha. Meri duniya uske saamne khadi thi… Aur wo ja rahi thi.

Main uske pair pakad ke baith gaya. Aankhon me aansu the. Voice break ho rahi thi. “Please mat jao…”

Usne apna pair peeche khinch liya. Aur sirf ek line boli —

“It’s over”

Bas. Itna hi. Na koi drama. Na koi emotion. Bas ek full stop.

Wo waha se chali gayi. Aur main wahi reh gaya. Log dheere dheere apne kaam me lag gaye. Road fir normal ho gayi. Par mere andar sab kuch abnormal ho chuka tha.

Us din ke baad bhi main rukha nahi. Agla din fir gaya. Phir agla. Phir agla. 6 din tak lagatar. Har din same cheez. Wait. Request. Begging. Aur end me rejection.

Har din meri self-respect thodi aur kam hoti gayi. Har din main thoda aur toot gaya. Par fir bhi main ruk nahi pa raha tha. Kyuki main usse pyaar karta tha… Ya shayad… Main uski aadat se bahar nahi aa pa raha tha.

6th din… Main uske saamne khada tha. Par is baar maine kuch nahi bola. Bas usse dekha. Aur us moment me mujhe samajh aa gaya… Wo wapas nahi aane wali.

Uski aankhon me ab mere liye kuch nahi tha. Na gussa. Na pyaar. Na care. Bas… Nothing.

Aur shayad… “Nothing” sabse painful feeling hoti hai.

Us din main ghar aaya. Room me gaya. Aur bed par let gaya. Ceiling ko dekhte hue ek hi thought aaya — “Sab khatam ho gaya”. Aur is baar… Sach me khatam ho gaya tha.

08

Collapse

Breakup ke baad log bolte hain — “Time sab thik kar deta hai”. Par sach kya hai? Time tab tak kuch thik nahi karta… Jab tak insaan khud thik hone ki koshish na kare. Aur main? Main koshish hi nahi kar raha tha.

Uske jaane ke baad meri life ruk si gayi. Same room… Same bed… Same log… Par sab kuch alag lag raha tha. Jaise duniya chal rahi hai… Aur main wahi atak gaya hoon.

Subah uthta tha… Par uthne ka reason nahi hota tha. Phone uthata tha… Par check karne ke liye koi message nahi hota tha. Uska “Good morning”… Uska “Kya kar rahe ho”… Sab gayab ho chuka tha.

Main din bhar bas uski chats read karta rehta. Old messages. Old jokes. Old “Betu”. Har line padhta… Aur har line ke saath ek hi thought aata — “Ye sab sach tha na?”

Kabhi kabhi lagta tha… Wo abhi message karegi. “Sorry”. “Wapas aajao”. Par phir reality hit karti… Wo aisa nahi karegi.

Maine padhai completely ignore kar di. Books khol kar baithta tha… Par 5 minute baad hi mind wapas usi taraf chala jata. Words dikhte the… Samajh nahi aate the.

Coding jo meri strength thi… Wo bhi chhoot gayi. Laptop open karta… Par screen blank lagti. Jaise dimaag me kuch process hi nahi ho raha.

Mere dost notice karne lage the. “Tu thik hai?” wo puchte. Main har baar same answer deta — “Haan”. Par wo “haan” jhoot tha. Aur mujhe khud pata tha.

Body bhi react karne lagi thi. Kabhi bina reason ke weakness feel hoti. Kabhi school me khade khade chakkar aa jata. Ek baar to main literally class me gir gaya.

Ghar wale tension me aa gaye. Mujhe doctor ke paas le gaye. Tests hue. Reports normal aayi. Doctor ne bola, “Stress hai”. Par unhe nahi pata tha… Ye sirf stress nahi tha. Ye andar ka collapse tha.

Main ghar me zyada time apne room me rehne laga. Door band. Light off. Bas main… aur meri soch. Kabhi kabhi itna overthinking hota tha… Ki dimaag literally heavy feel karta tha.

Main situations imagine karta rehta. “Agar main us din aisa na karta…” “Agar main usse space deta…” “Agar main control na karta…” Har “agar” mujhe aur deep le ja raha tha.

Sabse dangerous cheez kya thi? Main khud ko blame kar raha tha. Har cheez ke liye. “Sab meri galti hai”. “Main hi toxic tha”. “Main hi reason hoon”.

Aur sach ye hai… Main partially galat tha. Par poora nahi. Par us waqt mujhe ye balance samajh nahi aa raha tha. Main sirf extreme me soch raha tha.

Ek din mere ek close dost ne mere parents ko sab bata diya. Usne bola, “Ye sab ek ladki ki wajah se hai”. Us moment ke baad… Sab change ho gaya.

Mere parents ka behaviour change hone laga. Pehle wo mujhe samajhne ki koshish karte the… Ab wo bas distance rakhne lage. Maa jo pehle har cheez me care karti thi… Ab sirf khana dene aati thi. Baaki baat kam ho gayi.

Mujhe unki aankhon me disappointment dikhne laga. Aur wo feeling… Aur tod deti hai.

Main aur zyada isolate ho gaya. Na friends se baat. Na family se. Na kisi aur se.

Kabhi kabhi aisa lagta tha… Main room me hoon… Par mind kahi aur hai. Jaise main apni life ko bahar se dekh raha hoon. Control nahi hai.

Main khud ko mirror me dekhta… Aur sochta — “Ye kaun hai?” Wo confident ladka jo coding karta tha… Jo apni life me focused tha… Wo kahi gayab ho gaya tha.

Uski jagah ek aisa insaan khada tha… Jo sirf past me jee raha tha.

Raat sabse worst hoti thi. Kyuki distraction zero hota tha. Aur thoughts maximum. Main bed par leta rehta… Ceiling ko dekhta rehta… Aur uske baare me sochta rehta. Uski smile. Uski awaaz. Uska “Betu”.

Kabhi kabhi aankhon me aansu aate… Kabhi nahi bhi aate. Par andar se ek constant heaviness rehti thi.

Mujhe lagne laga… “Shayad main kabhi normal nahi ho paunga”. “Shayad main yahi reh jaunga”. Aur sabse dangerous thought kya tha? “Main khud ko kho chuka hoon”.

Par sach ye tha… Main khud ko khoya nahi tha. Main bas… Khud se door ho gaya tha. Aur difference bahut bada hota hai. Khoya hua mil sakta hai… Par jo door chala jaye… Usse wapas lana padta hai.

Aur us waqt… Main us effort ke liye ready nahi tha.

09

Move On

Time beet raha tha… Par mere andar kuch change nahi ho raha tha. Log bolte hain — “Move on kar jao”. Par move on ek button nahi hota… Jise press kiya aur sab thik ho gaya.

Main ab bhi usse bhool nahi pa raha tha. Har din ek hi thought ke saath start hota — “Kaash aaj uska message aa jaye”. Aur har raat ek hi thought ke saath end hota — “Aaj bhi nahi aaya”.

Maine usse contact karne ki bohot koshish ki. Har possible way try kiya. Naye numbers… Naye accounts… Har baar ek hi hope ke saath message karta — “Please ek baar baat kar lo”. Par har baar same result. Block. Ignore. Silence.

Shuru me gussa aata tha. Phir frustration. Phir sadness. Aur finally… Thakan.

Ek time aisa aaya… Jab main khud se puchne laga — “Main kar kya raha hoon?” “Main kiske peeche bhaag raha hoon?” “Ek aise insaan ke… jo clearly aage badh chuka hai?”

Par fir bhi… Dil aur dimaag ek page par nahi the. Dimaag bolta — “Stop”. Dil bolta — “Ek last try aur”. Aur insaan aksar… Dil ki sunta hai.

Ek din mujhe uski ek nayi ID dikhi. Mujhe nahi pata kaise… Par wo meri screen par thi. Jaise kismat ne last baar chance diya ho.

Maine kuch seconds tak us profile ko dekha. Socha… “Message karu ya nahi?” Andar se awaaz aayi — “Ye last hai”.

Maine chat open ki. Typing start ki. Is baar koi lambi baat nahi likhi. Bas ek simple line —

Please wapas aa jao

Send. Message bhejne ke baad main phone ko dekhta raha. Par is baar feeling alag thi. Pehle anxiety hoti thi… Is baar bas silence tha. Jaise main already answer jaanta hoon.

Kuch time baad reply aaya. Short. Cold. Distance se bhara hua. Us reply me ek hi message tha —

Move on.

Bas. Na koi explanation. Na koi emotion. Na koi past. Na koi “Betu”.

Aur us moment me… Sab clear ho gaya.

Main screen ko dekh raha tha… Par aankhon me aansu nahi the. Shock nahi tha. Pain bhi alag type ka tha. Ye wo pain tha… Jahan insaan ro bhi nahi pata. Kyuki wo already itna ro chuka hota hai… Ki andar kuch bacha hi nahi hota.

Maine chat close ki. Phone side me rakha. Aur pehli baar… Main chup chaap baitha raha. Na overthinking. Na imagination. Na “what if”. Bas ek thought aaya — “Ye sach me khatam ho gaya”. Aur is baar… Main accept kar raha tha.

Mujhe realise hua… Main usse nahi, us memory se chipka hua tha. Us version se… Jo ab exist hi nahi karta. Wo ladki jisse main pyaar karta tha… Wo shayad ab hai hi nahi. Ya phir… Wo kabhi thi hi nahi. Aur sabse important baat… Main bhi wo insaan nahi raha… Jo us waqt tha.

Main dheere dheere uth kar mirror ke paas gaya. Khud ko dekha. Kaafi time baad… Properly. Aankhon ke neeche dark circles the. Face thoda dull tha. Par ek cheez nayi thi… Clarity.

Maine dheere se khud se bola — “Bas. Ab aur nahi.”

Us din maine decide kiya — Main usse force karke wapas nahi laa sakta. Main past change nahi kar sakta. Par main future bana sakta hoon. Aur shayad… Yahi real move on hota hai.

Main wapas apni life build karunga. Step by step. Slowly. Par is baar… Kisi aur ke liye nahi. Khud ke liye.

Uski yaadein shayad kabhi puri tarah nahi jayengi. Par ab wo mujhe todengi nahi… Bas yaad dilayengi — Ki main kabhi kitna deeply feel kar sakta tha.

Aur shayad… Ek din main usse thank you bolunga. Kyuki usne mujhe pyaar nahi sikhaya… Usne mujhe khud ko samajhna sikhaya.

Aur kahani yahi khatam nahi hoti… Bas yaha se… Ek nayi shuruat hoti hai.

10

Beginning

Sach kya hai? Sach ye hai ki maine haar nahi maani thi. Maine har wo cheez try ki jo main kar sakta tha. Har wo advice follow ki jo logon ne di. Kyuki mujhe lagta tha — “Agar main aur try karunga… to shayad wo wapas aa jayegi”.

Kisi ne bola, “Uski friends ke saamne kisi aur ladki se baat kar… jealous hogi”. Maine wo bhi try kiya. Main uske saamne normal banne ki acting karta… Dusri ladkiyon se baat karta… Par andar se? Main sirf usse dekh raha hota tha. Ek chhoti si reaction ke liye. Par usne react hi nahi kiya. Na gussa. Na jealousy. Bas… Ignore.

Kisi ne bola, “Thoda strong ban… thoda attitude dikha… thoda dhamka”. Maine wo bhi try kiya. Maine usse thoda rude tone me baat ki. Thoda distance create karne ki koshish ki. Par sach ye tha… Wo main tha hi nahi. Aur wo fake version zyada der tik nahi paya. Kyuki jahan real feelings hoti hain… Waha acting fail ho jati hai.

Kisi ne bola, “Maafi mang le… dil pighal jayega”. Aur ye to main already kar chuka tha. Main uske saamne ro chuka tha. Haath jod chuka tha. Apni ego tod chuka tha. Apni self-respect side me rakh chuka tha.

Par ek cheez maine bohot late samjhi… Kisi ko wapas lane ke liye jo kuch bhi main kar raha tha… Wo sab uske liye nahi tha. Wo sab… Mere liye tha.

Main usse nahi chah raha tha… Main us feeling ko chah raha tha. Wo attention. Wo importance. Wo “Betu”.

Aur jab wo sab chala gaya… To main usse wapas laana chahta tha. Chahe wo khush ho ya na ho. Yahi meri sabse badi galti thi.

Pyaar me hum sochte hain — “Agar main itna feel karta hoon… to wo bhi karegi”. Par reality simple hai — Feelings force nahi ki ja sakti.

Usne choose kiya — move on. Aur maine choose kiya — hold on. Aur dono choices alag directions me thi.

Main har din sochta tha — “Bas ek chance aur mil jaye”. Par maine kabhi ye nahi socha… “Wo chance lena bhi chahti hai ya nahi?”

Sach ye bhi hai… Main usse pyaar karta tha. Par utna hi sach ye bhi hai… Main possessive tha. Controlling tha. Aur kabhi kabhi… Selfish bhi.

Maine usse protect karne ke naam par restrict kiya. Care ke naam par pressure diya. Aur pyaar ke naam par… Usse thakaya. Aur shayad isi liye… Wo chali gayi.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki wo perfect thi. Na hi iska matlab ye hai ki main poora galat tha. Par iska matlab ye zaroor hai… Ki main perfect nahi tha. Aur ye accept karna… Sabse tough part hota hai.

Aaj main usse blame nahi karta. Na usse hate karta hoon. Na usse wapas chahta hoon. Aaj main bas samajhta hoon. Ki wo chapter meri life ka important part tha. Par permanent nahi.

Aur sabse bada truth kya hai? Main usse nahi haara… Main khud se haara tha.

Aur ab… Main wahi game dobara nahi haarunga. Main ab pyaar se nahi darunga… Par main khud ko bhi nahi khona dunga.

Kyuki ab mujhe pata hai — Kisi aur ko paane se pehle… Khud ko sambhalna zaroori hota hai.

Aur shayad… Yahi meri kahani ka asli end nahi… Asli beginning hai.
